PEPPY - one-click Slippi friendlies (early test build)
=====================================================

WHAT IT IS
Peppy sets up Slippi direct matches for you. No typing connect codes in-game,
no scrolling menus: the game opens straight into your match, already connected,
with your character and stage picked. You just play.

WHAT YOU NEED FIRST
  1. Slippi Launcher installed, logged in, with your Melee ISO configured.
  2. Press PLAY in the Slippi Launcher once, so Slippi downloads its game files.
  3. Your GameCube controller / adapter set up the way you normally play.

HOW TO RUN IT
  Unzip anywhere, then run Peppy.exe.

  Windows will probably warn you ("Windows protected your PC") because this
  build is not code-signed - it's a hobby app, not a commercial release.
  Click "More info" then "Run anyway". You can verify what it does: the whole
  thing is plain readable JavaScript inside the resources folder.

  You may also get a Windows Firewall prompt for "Slippi Dolphin". Allow it -
  that's Melee's netplay, same as normal Slippi.

HOW TO PLAY SOMEONE
  1. Type YOUR connect code in the top-right box (e.g. BIRD#704).
  2. Pick your character and your game-1 stage.
  3. Type your friend's code under PLAY SOMEONE and hit CHALLENGE.
  4. Your friend does the same, with YOUR code.
  5. Both hit "I'M READY".
  Melee opens hidden, connects, and appears once you're actually in the match.

WHAT IS AND ISN'T FINISHED
  Working : direct matches, automatic character + game-1 stage selection,
            hidden setup, recently-played list, add-a-friend list (local).
  Not yet : the shared Fort Wayne queue (needs the Peppy server), Discord
            notifications, tournaments, best-of-3/5 sets.

WHAT IT DOES TO YOUR PC
  * Copies your Slippi Dolphin into %APPDATA%\Peppy (your real Slippi install
    is never modified).
  * Writes match settings + game patches into that copy only.
  * Never asks for, reads, or sends your slippi.gg password. Your Slippi login
    file is used only where Dolphin already expects it, on your own machine.
  * No account, no telemetry, nothing leaves your computer in this build.

TO UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder you unzipped, and delete %APPDATA%\Peppy.

IF SOMETHING BREAKS
  The status bar at the bottom of the window says what's wrong (usually Slippi
  or the ISO not being found). Tell whoever sent you this build - early test
  feedback is the whole point.
